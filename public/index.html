Headmasters' Portal User Guide & Procedure

Step-by-step procedure for School Headmasters & Principals to complete DCEB processes:

1

Calculate Student Strength & Fee

Check the official DCEB Fee Structure 2026-27 tab to calculate total fee payable based on class enrollment (Classes I to X).

2

Online Fee Remittance

Click on Direct Fee Payment Link to pay via SBI Collect / Treasury Portal. Download the DU Reference Payment Receipt.

3

Apply for TC Books & Question Papers

Submit the online application in TC BOOK Application tab with your School UDISE Code and Payment Receipt Number.

4

Follow Academic Schedule & Mission March 2027

Download syllabus breakups, timetable, and model papers under Academic Calendars and Mission March 2027.

MISSION MARCH 2027 PROGRAMME

A target-driven academic initiative designed by DCEB Sri Sathya Sai District to achieve 100% success and high GPAs in SSC Public Examinations March 2027.

1

Special Remedial Classes

Dedicated 1-hour morning and evening preparation sessions for C & D grade students based on baseline assessments.

Weekly Slip Tests

3

3-Stage Pre-Public Exams
